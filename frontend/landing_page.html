Fig. 06 / Live quality checks

See quality issues while there’s time to respond.

TaskGlass checks answers on gold items against known answers and compares independent annotators on the same item. Both readings update as work comes in, giving you a reason to look more closely at a person, item, or instruction.

Item 018 / region comparison
Human annotationGold reference
87%

Consistency across people.

If people agree with each other but disagree with gold, inspect the reference and instructions. If people disagree with each other, inspect the item and labeling rules. The scores guide review; they do not determine the cause on their own.
